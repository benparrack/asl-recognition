          Welcome to SignDesk

          How it works

          • Letters: hold a handshape steady until the ring fills and it's added. Trace J or Z in the air. Lower your hand to end a word.
          • Words: raise your hands, sign, then lower them (or hold still). The best match is added; press 1–3 to swap it for another match.
          • Two-handed signs: sit back so you're visible from head to waist, with the camera at face height, and sign at chest height. With your hands close to the camera, often only one gets detected.
          • Practice scores you on prompts without touching your text.
